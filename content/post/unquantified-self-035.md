The Summer was hot, way too hot for me and I ended up spending most of those
long awaited holidays at home. No luck.

I didn't do anything special except that I followed my son's advice and tried
the last version of BetterMC. It ran smoothly on my MacBook Pro M4 and it was a
fun journey (9d18h of playtime). He joined me to fight Ignis and we ended up
with full ignitium and diamong-netherite or dragon gears.

{{< figure src="/img/2026-08-02_15.55.52.png" >}}

Besides, I decided to cleanup and move my backup from a very old Time Capsule to
more modern SSD storage, with deduplication on another external HD. As I was in
the mood of cleaning things up, I also archived a lot of stuff that I will
rpobably no longer need. I also bought some macOS apps which I find quite handy
as a complement to my cli-based workflow: Downie, Permute and Meta instead of
yt-dlp, convert from Imagemagick, ffmpeg or id3tags; Zipic instead of
ImageOptim; TableFlip for the case I'm not in Vim with vim-table-mode; News
Explorer and bIRC which I already reviewed in recent posts; MarkChart to edit
Mermaid diagram when I'm not using Monodraw; Audio Hijack and Fission for
everything sound-related. I also renewed my Mathematica (sorry, we should say
Wolfram now) license and subscribed to a cheap one for Stata 19 (I'm running on
low budget at the Uni).

This was also the occasion to listen to a lot of music and watch some good old
movies that I haven't watched before.

- **Music:** electronic, dark and cold wave, and synthpop almost exclusively.
  I've discovered new singers or bands, and this is great. I may implement a
  tracking system with a summary statistics of sort because it becomes hard to
  summarize everything from memory. I no longer use any scrobbler, and Apple
  Music Replay seems a bit limited and require to log in on Safari. Anyway, here
  it goes, very briefly and in no particular order: Plastique noir, Cold Cave,
  Gothic Tropic, Paradox Obscur, Sexual Purity, Ductape, Forever Grey, Light
  Asylum, Tempers.

- **Movies:** many movies from David Lynch and some other random stuff. I was
  afraid at first to have to watch movies that last more than 2h30, but that was
  okay in retrospect. I particularly liked My Blueberry Nights, Blue Velvet and
  Lost Highway. I've already watched In the Mood for Love in the past, as well
  as Reservoir Dogs, Ronin, Inland Empire and Spy Game.
