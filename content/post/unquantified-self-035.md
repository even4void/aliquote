The Summer was hot, way too hot for me and I ended up spending most of those long awaited holidays at home. No luck.

I didn't do anything special except that I followed my son's advice and tried the last version of BetterMC. It ran smoothly on my MacBook Pro M4 and it was a fun journey (9d18h of playtime). He joined me to fight Ignis and we ended up with full ignitium and diamong-netherite or dragon gears.

Besides, I decided to cleanup and move my backup from a very old Time Capsule to more modern SSD storage, with deduplication on another external HD. As I was in the mood of cleaning things up, I also archived a lot of stuff that I will rpobably no longer need. I also bought some macOS apps which I find quite handy as a complement to my cli-based workflow: Downie, Permute and Meta instead of yt-dlp, convert from Imagemagick, ffmpeg or id3tags; Zipic instead of ImageOptim; TableFlip for the case I'm not in Vim with vim-table-mode; News Explorer and bIRC which I already reviewed in recent posts; MarkChart to edit Mermaid diagram when I'm not using Monodraw; Audio Hijack and Fission for everything sound-related. I also renewed my Mathematica (sorry, we should say Wolfram now) license and subscribed to a cheap one for Stata 19 (I'm running on low budget at the Uni).
