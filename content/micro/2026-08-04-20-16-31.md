Thanks to Armin Briegel, it is easy to replace Google translation cli tool with
a proper [Swift](https://github.com/scriptingosx/translate-cli) utility that
relies on builtin Apple Translate and local language assets.

![img](/img/2026-08-04-19-05-11.png)

Bonus: Put this in your Vim Markdown plugin and you can replace a visual
selection with its translation to the appropriate language (here, English):

```vim
xnoremap <buffer> <silent> grN :!translate --to en<cr>
```
